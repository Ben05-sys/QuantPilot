# Contributor Licence Agreement

## Why this exists

QuantPilot is published under AGPL-3.0 and that is not going to change. This
agreement does not take your work private and it does not take your copyright.

What it does is let the project be licensed on other terms *as well* — a
commercial licence alongside the AGPL one, so the terminal can be sold or
hosted without the network clause applying to the buyer. A project cannot do
that unless one party holds the right to license every line of it. Without an
agreement like this, a single merged pull request from someone unreachable
locks the whole project into AGPL-only forever, and that decision gets made by
accident rather than on purpose.

**You keep the copyright in your contribution.** The grant below is a licence,
not an assignment. Your code stays yours: you can relicense it, sell it, put it
in another project, or do anything else you like with it. You are giving
permission, not handing something over.

If that trade is not one you want to make, say so in the pull request. A change
that only makes sense as AGPL-only is still worth discussing — it just cannot
be merged into this repository.

Signing once covers everything you contribute from then on. You do not need to
sign again for a second pull request.

## How to sign

Include this line in the description of your first pull request, with your own
name and the date:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it. Signed: <your name>, <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

The checkbox in the pull request template records the same thing. Your GitHub
account and the commit history are the record of who agreed and when.

One signature covers all of your future contributions. If you contribute under
more than one identity — a personal account and a work account, say — sign from
each.

## Contributing on behalf of a company

If you are contributing as part of your employment, or your employer otherwise
has rights to what you write, the individual agreement above is not enough on
its own — your employer has to be the one granting the licence. Open an issue
titled `Corporate CLA` and the Project Owner will arrange one. Do not submit
employer-owned work until that is in place.
